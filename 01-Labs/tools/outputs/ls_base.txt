Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the sunshine. One day, she found a shiny rock and decided to keep it under her pillow. She thought it was a perfect place to keep it.

Lily's friend, Tommy, saw her playing with the rock. "Wow, that's a cool rock! Can I keep it?" asked Lily. "No, I can't," said Tommy. "You have your own special rock."

Lily felt sad for Tommy. She didn't want to share her own rock by the river. "Why don't you keep the rock and share it with your friends?" asked Lily. "I think you should share it with me," said Tommy.
