=== 4. The same meaning in English and Chinese: how many tokens? ===

English: The little cat is playing in the garden.
Chinese: 小猫在花园里玩。
  this lab's tokenizer (English stories only, vocab 4096): English 9 tokens, Chinese 24 tokens
  GPT-2 tokenizer (vocab 50,257): English 9 tokens, Chinese 17 tokens
  GPT-4o tokenizer (vocab about 200,000): English 9 tokens, Chinese 8 tokens

English: Thank you very much for your help today.
Chinese: 非常感谢你今天的帮助。
  this lab's tokenizer (English stories only, vocab 4096): English 9 tokens, Chinese 33 tokens
  GPT-2 tokenizer (vocab 50,257): English 9 tokens, Chinese 21 tokens
  GPT-4o tokenizer (vocab about 200,000): English 9 tokens, Chinese 7 tokens
