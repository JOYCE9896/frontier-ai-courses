' strawbery'       -> ␣straw | ber | y
' hippopotamus'    -> ␣h | ipp | op | ot | am | us
' 2026'            -> ␣ | 2 | 0 | 2 | 6
' 12345'           -> ␣ | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5
' Kallini'         -> ␣K | all | in | i
