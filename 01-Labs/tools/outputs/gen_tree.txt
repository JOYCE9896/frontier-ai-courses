=== 开头：The old tree in the forest（温度 0.8）===
The old tree in the forest, and the old man stayed there. He was so scared that he started to laugh.

The old tree had a big smile on its face. It said hello and asked the old man, "What's wrong here?" The old man replied, "My voice has come from me and I think your voice was calling me. I'm here to take you away from me". The old man said, "My little voice is coming from here. I belong to a hidden place and now I can't find you."

The old man was so kind. He said, "It's only a strange place, and I can find you again."

The old man and the old man walked around the world and had lots of fun. They soon became the best of friends. The old man was reminded of his magical friend.
