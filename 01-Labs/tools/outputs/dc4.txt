Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to play outside in the winter. One day, Mia's friend, Tom, came to play with her. Tom said, "Mia, let's play a game. Let's play a game."

Mia and Tom played a game. They ran, jumped, and laughed. Mia had so much fun. She was happy that Tom could play the game. Mia was happy that Tom was not shy. From that day on, Mia and Tom played together every day.
