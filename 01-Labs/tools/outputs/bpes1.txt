=== 1. Text is stored as bytes ===
'a'    -> 1 character(s), 1 byte(s): [97]
'cat'  -> 3 character(s), 3 byte(s): [99, 97, 116]
'é'    -> 1 character(s), 2 byte(s): [195, 169]
'地'    -> 1 character(s), 3 byte(s): [229, 156, 176]
'猫咪'   -> 2 character(s), 6 byte(s): [231, 140, 171, 229, 146, 170]
'😀'    -> 1 character(s), 4 byte(s): [240, 159, 152, 128]
