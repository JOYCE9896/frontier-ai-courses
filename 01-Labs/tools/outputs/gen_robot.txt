=== 开头：Once upon a time, there was a little robot（温度 0.8）===
Once upon a time, there was a little robot named Bob. Bob loved to play with his friends. One day, he saw a big, shiny truck on the ground. The truck was not good for Bob. Bob wanted to play with his truck, but his friend, a dog named Sam, was wrong. Sam said, "Bob, let me play with this truck. It is very noisy."

Bob said, "But it is also fun. I like to play with it and beat things to do." Sam agreed, and they played with the truck together. They had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, Bob said, "Thank you for playing with me. I learned how to play with my truck."

Sam and Sam played with the truck together and had a great day.
